# Engagement, durable learning, and behavioral mechanisms

**Status:** Investigative research. The synthesis records findings and recommendations; it does not by itself authorize product changes.

This directory preserves the August 2026 independent research program on how Externalize can improve voluntary return and persistence without corrupting durable learning, transfer, calibration, or mastery evidence.

## Research question

> How can Externalize exploit the strongest known mechanisms for motivation, habit formation, attention, repeated engagement, memory, and learning while preserving—or improving—durable retention, transfer, calibration, and genuine mastery?

Governing principle:

> **Engagement is instrumental. Learning is the objective.**

## Epistemic status

The reports are evidence and candidate reasoning, not authority. Agreement among the three models is not treated as evidence. The synthesis distinguishes:

- **Known project state** — current Externalize behavior or already-authorized project direction.
- **Research findings** — conclusions supported by the reviewed evidence.
- **Open questions** — material uncertainties that remain unresolved.
- **Recommendations** — proposed next investigations or design directions.
- **Decisions** — only explicit owner-authorized commitments. None of the research reports independently creates one.

The baseline validation period should remain uncontaminated by an untested engagement layer until evidence identifies an actual return/persistence problem worth solving.
